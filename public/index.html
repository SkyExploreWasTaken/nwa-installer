Nwa Toolkit

  • No account

  • Chrome & Edge
1

Calculator

Plug the calculator in, unlock it, then connect.

2

Update the calculator's software

This calculator runs Epsilon 15 or older, which has no space for .nwa apps (they need Epsilon 16 or later), so it must be updated first. Nothing is written without you: pick its model, then a version, then confirm.

Which model is it?

The exact model is printed on the back of the calculator (e.g. N0110 or N0115).
